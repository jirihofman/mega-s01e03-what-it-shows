Separate engineering work

Parser: the shared parser reads month first for day-first tenants. For example, Harbour Dental’s R-41200 reports 7 April becoming 4 July. Correct parsing at the boundary using the declared tenant date format or an unambiguous date representation; reject ambiguous unsupported inputs rather than guessing. Verify app, email and booking-v2 paths, and booking-v3 non-form paths. Use unambiguous dates as well as dates with both fields at most twelve in regression coverage.

Form hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY although its day-first tenant form interprets input day first. This affects form reports from Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour. R-41230 and R-41241 are examples. Correct the displayed hint and the form’s interpretation together, using the tenant format. A parser-only fix does not repair this misleading instruction. Check a day-first booking-v3 form separately from the same tenant’s app channel.

File one GitHub issue for each cause, with the computed count and report-ID membership. Counts describe classification under the supplied model, not independently reproduced production defects. Keep the one-day-move reports in the parser count, flag their symptom for investigation, and do not claim a timezone or daylight-saving cause without evidence.

Received is different from merged

The July merge spike reflects desk processing of older reports. The received series already begins in April and continues into October. A merge timestamp measures triage activity, not when the defect began. A queued fix and a “known” status are not evidence of recovery.

Triage from now on

  1. Resolve the tenant and inspect its locale, form version and report channel.
  2. Compute a candidate cause and read the report to check whether the symptom fits. Record contrary evidence.
  3. Merge only into a ticket with the same established cause; preserve each distinct report ID and count it once.
  4. Keep parser and hint tickets separate even when subjects match. Different subjects may still share a cause.
  5. For a missing tenant or unexplained symptom, leave the report unclassified for investigation. Two unknown causes do not justify merging.
  6. Recompute counts as the desk receives reports. Review the rule when form versions or the classification model change.

Review and consequences

The old triage page’s review date triggers the shared layout’s stale warning. That warning is a reason to verify its guidance against executable evidence. The incident page is more recent, yet its prose also disagrees with its own rendered cause table. Fresh metadata alone is insufficient.
